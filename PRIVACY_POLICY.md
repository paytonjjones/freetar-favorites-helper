# Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2026-06-01

Freetar Favorites Helper is a Chrome extension that transfers saved Ultimate Guitar tabs into Freetar.

## Data the extension handles

The extension processes:
- your Ultimate Guitar favorites data from the currently logged-in `My tabs` page
- the exported favorites JSON file on your device
- the favorites data written into Freetar in your browser

## How the data is used

The extension uses that data only to:
- read your saved tabs from Ultimate Guitar
- generate a Freetar-compatible export file
- import that file into Freetar
- clear favorites from Freetar when you choose the Advanced clear action

## Data sharing

The extension does not send your favorites data to any server operated by the developer.
It does not sell, rent, or share your data with third parties.

## Storage

The extension does not maintain its own backend or account system.
Any favorites data is handled locally in your browser or in files you choose to download and import.

## Contact

If you have questions about this policy, use the repository issues or contact the maintainer through the project page.
